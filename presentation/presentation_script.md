**Presenter**: Lead Data Analyst

**Target Audience**: Executive Judges & Leadership Team

## Slide 2: Logistics Bottlenecks & Geographic Realities (0:45 - 1:30)

### Visual Layout:
- **Header**: Geographic Disparities & Shipping Realities
- **Left Panel**: Chart Figure `q3_seller_pareto_concentration.png` showing Top 10% sellers generate 67.5% of GMV.
- **Right Panel**: Chart Figure `q3_regional_freight_and_leadtime.png` comparing Inter-state vs Intra-state freight & lead times.

### Spoken Script:
> "While top-line growth is strong, our geographic analysis uncovers significant operational friction. 
> 
> First, revenue is highly concentrated: **Top 10% of sellers generate 67.5% of total marketplace GMV**. Second, **64% of all shipments are inter-state cross-country orders**. 
> 
> Because sellers are heavily concentrated in the Southeast state of São Paulo while buyers are spread across all 27 Brazilian states, inter-state packages suffer **+74% higher freight costs**—R$ 27 vs R$ 15—and take **15.1 days to deliver**, nearly double the intra-state duration of 7.9 days. Regions like the North and Northeast face average lead times exceeding 18 days."

---

## Slide 3: Customer Experience & Root-Cause Breakdown (1:30 - 2:15)

### Visual Layout:
- **Header**: Delivery Lateness: The Primary Dissatisfaction Driver
- **Left Panel**: Chart Figure `q2_review_score_vs_delivery_lateness.png` showing review score crash from 4.28 to 2.55.
- **Right Panel**: Chart Figure `q6_complaint_text_themes.png` showing 58.4% of low review comments cite logistics/non-arrival.

### Spoken Script:
> "This brings us to the core root cause of customer dissatisfaction. Overall, Olist achieves a **91.9% on-time delivery rate**. On-time orders earn an impressive **4.28 out of 5 stars**, with only 6.8% 1-star reviews. 
> 
> However, when an order is delivered late—which happens in **8.1% of cases**—the review score collapses to **2.55 stars**, and the 1-star review rate explodes by **+587% to 46.6%**. 
> 
> Text mining over 25,000 low-rating comments confirms this empirical finding: **58.4% of all 1- and 2-star comments explicitly cite non-delivery or shipping delays**, far outweighing product defects at 24.7%."

---

## Slide 4: Strategic Recommendations & Business Impact (2:15 - 3:00)

### Visual Layout:
- **Header**: Actionable Roadmap for Sustainable Scaling
- **Three Pillar Cards**:
  1. **Regional Fulfillment Hubs**: Move top 10% seller inventory closer to buyers in S/NE.
  2. **Automated Dispatch Timer**: Enforce 48-hour carrier handoff SLA for sellers.
  3. **Promotional Financing**: Promote 4-6 credit card installments for high-AOV categories.

### Spoken Script:
> "To solve these challenges and drive profitable scaling, we recommend three actionable initiatives:
> 
> First, **Establish Regional Fulfillment Hubs** for our top 10% sellers. Positioning high-velocity inventory in regional hubs will cut inter-state lead times by 5 days and reduce late delivery risk below 5%.
> 
> Second, **Enforce Automated Seller Dispatch SLAs**. Over 14,000 orders experienced seller dispatch lags exceeding 5 days. Implementing a strict 48-hour carrier handoff rule will protect ~1,800 orders annually from 1-star ratings.
> 
> Third, **Leverage Payment Financing**. Credit cards drive 78.4% of revenue, and orders using 4 to 6 installments achieve 45% higher basket sizes. Offering zero-interest promotional installments in home and tech categories will boost platform AOV by 8 to 12%.
> 
> Thank you! I welcome your questions."
