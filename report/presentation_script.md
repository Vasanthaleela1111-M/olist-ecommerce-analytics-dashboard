# Olist Executive Presentation: Spoken Script (3:00 Minutes)

**Speaker Target Audience**: Olist Operations and Customer Experience Leadership  
**Tone**: Authoritative, executive-ready, evidence-backed, precise  
**Pacing**: 135–140 words per minute (Total Word Count: ~420 words)

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] Section 1: Business Problem (25 Seconds)

*"Good morning, leadership team.*

*Olist has achieved remarkable marketplace growth, expanding order volume by **714 percent**—from 788 to over 7,380 orders per month. However, behind our 4.07-star platform average lies a critical operational challenge: **15.09 percent** of orders—over 15,000 transactions—ended in 1 or 2-star reviews.* 

*In fact, 1-star ratings account for **78.6 percent** of all customer dissatisfaction. Our customers aren't mildly disappointed; when fulfillment breaks down, their experience collapses completely."*

---

### [0:25 – 0:50] Section 2: Analytical Approach (25 Seconds)

*"To fix this, we conducted an exhaustive analysis of all 99,441 platform orders, maintaining 100 percent financial accuracy across 15.84 million Reais in gross revenue.*

*We controlled for multi-item joins, split payments, and delivery survivorship bias. By evaluating relative risk ratios against our platform baseline, we isolated the exact operational bottlenecks driving bad reviews without making unsupported causal assumptions."*

---

### [0:50 – 2:10] Section 3: Three Strongest Findings (80 Seconds)

*"Our findings reveal three clear operational friction points:*

*First, **the late delivery review penalty is immediate and non-linear**. On-time deliveries record a low-rating rate of just **9.49 percent**. But the moment an order passes its promised date, the low-rating rate jumps to **54.64 percent**—a **3.6-times risk multiplier**. At four or more days late, three out of four customers leave 1-star reviews.*

*Second, **interstate shipping bottlenecks degrade key regional corridors**. Because **70.6 percent** of our sellers are concentrated in São Paulo, 64 percent of all orders cross state lines. On our largest interstate corridor—São Paulo to Rio de Janeiro—lateness hits **14.99 percent**, driving low ratings up to **22.57 percent**.*

*Third, **heavy goods face a specialized fulfillment crisis**. While standard categories like health and beauty thrive at 4.17 stars, office furniture averages a low **3.62 stars** with a **22.63 percent** low-rating rate. Heavy items incur freight costs 136 percent above baseline and average 20.7 days in transit, with 57.5 percent of low ratings citing packaging damage or product defects."*

---

### [2:10 – 2:45] Section 4: Strategic Recommendations (35 Seconds)

*"We recommend three immediate, zero-CapEx operational interventions:*

1. *Inject **plus 3 to 4-day dynamic delivery buffers** into our shipping algorithm for cross-state orders into Rio de Janeiro, instantly eliminating artificial SLA breaches for 8,400 annual orders.*
2. *Enforce a **mandatory 3-day seller dispatch SLA**. Over 14,000 orders currently suffer merchant dispatch delays over 5 days, elevating low ratings to 22.26 percent.*
3. *Contract **specialized heavy-goods freight carriers** and enforce mandatory reinforced packaging for office furniture.*"

---

### [2:45 – 3:00] Section 5: Expected Business Impact & Closing (15 Seconds)

*"Olist does not have a demand or pricing problem—we have an avoidable fulfillment SLA problem.*

*Executing these three targeted actions will reduce our platform low-rating rate from **15.09 percent to under 11.5 percent** within six months, recovering high-ticket revenue and protecting long-term platform trust. Thank you."*
