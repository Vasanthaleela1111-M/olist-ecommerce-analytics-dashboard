Section 5: Three Highest-Priority Recommended Executive Actions

Action Plan Derived from Validated Empirical Findings & Audit Evidence
Action #1

Dynamic Delivery Date Buffer Adjustments for Cross-State Shipping (`SP -> RJ`)

Inject **+3 to +4 business day transit buffers** into estimated delivery dates for cross-state orders into Rio de Janeiro ($N = 8,431$). Eliminates artificial SLA breaches caused by carrier route delays.

• Projected Impact: Saves affected orders from 54.64% late low-rating risk down to 9.49% on-time baseline (-45.15 pp drop).
Action #2

Upstream Seller Dispatch SLA Enforcement (Mandatory 3-Day Limit)

Enforce strict seller carrier dispatch SLAs at Day 3. Automatically alert sellers at Day 2 and penalize slow dispatchers exceeding 5 days ($N = 14,180$ orders) to protect transit buffers.

• Projected Impact: Reduces low-rating probability by -9.84 pp (from 22.26% down to 12.42%) for slow-dispatch orders.
Action #3

Specialized Bulky-Goods Carrier Strategy for `office_furniture`

Partner with specialized heavy-goods logistics carriers, mandate reinforced packaging standards, and set realistic 20+ day delivery estimates for heavy furniture ($N = 7,615$ orders).

• Projected Impact: Recovers `office_furniture` satisfaction (3.62 stars → 4.07 stars) and protects R$ 273.2K GMV.
