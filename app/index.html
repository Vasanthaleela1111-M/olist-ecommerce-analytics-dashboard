Remediation resources must be focused 100% on fulfillment SLA enforcement and carrier handling rather than pricing adjustments or payment method restrictions. Delivery delay is the primary driver of bad reviews; freight ratios exhibit zero direct negative impact.

• Projected Impact: Saves affected orders from 54.64% late low-rating risk down to 9.49% on-time baseline (-45.15 pp drop).

Partner with specialized heavy-goods logistics carriers, mandate reinforced packaging standards, and set realistic 20+ day delivery estimates for heavy furniture (N = 7,615 orders).

• Projected Impact: Recovers office_furniture rating (3.62 stars → 4.07 stars) and protects R$ 273.2K GMV.

Data Methodology, Definitions & Audit Scope

1. Primary Analytical Grain

The central analytical grain of this platform is 1 row = 1 order (N = 99,441 master order rows). All relational joins across the 9 raw CSV datasets were aggregated to the order level prior to joining to prevent record multiplication or fan-out errors (100% audit pass across 6 merges with 0 duplicate rows).

2. Relational Aggregation Protocols

  • Item Aggregation (order_items): Item prices and freight costs were summed per order. Primary product category and seller state attributes were derived via deterministic primary item selection (.first()).
  • Payment Aggregation (order_payments): Total payment value was summed per order. Primary payment type was assigned based on highest value share, and installment count was assigned as the maximum installment value per order.
  • Review Aggregation (order_reviews): Joined 1:1 on order_id (N = 99,224 valid review scores).
  • Customer Tracking: Unique customer identity tracked via customer_unique_id to evaluate repeat purchase behavior.
  • Spatial Geolocation Deduplication: 5-digit CEP (ZIP) prefixes were deduplicated by computing spatial median latitude and longitude coordinates per prefix prior to location matching.

3. Delivered-Only Delivery Analysis Scoping

Delivery duration, transit lead times, and late delivery flags (is_late) were calculated strictly on the delivered order population (N = 96,470), excluding 2,971 canceled or undelivered orders to prevent missing timestamp bias.

4. Key Metric Definitions

  • Late Delivery Flag (is_late): 1 if order_delivered_customer_date > order_estimated_delivery_date, 0 otherwise.
  • Low Rating Flag (is_low_review): 1 if customer review score is 1 or 2 stars, 0 otherwise.
  • Freight Burden Ratio: total_freight / total_price.
  • Relative Risk Ratio: Segment Low Rating Rate / Platform Baseline Low Rating Rate (15.09%).

5. Strict Observational / Non-Causal Framing Disclaimer

All relationships displayed in this platform represent observational associations derived from historical transaction metadata. No causal claims are asserted without experimental counterfactuals.
